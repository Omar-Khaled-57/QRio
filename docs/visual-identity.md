# QRio visual identity

QRio is a light, privacy-first QR tool with a fresh green brand mark, mint
surfaces, and teal details. Its optional dark theme moves the same green accent
onto a deep forest background.

| Role | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | ![#f2f8f5](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%23f2f8f5?style=flat-square&color=f2f8f5) `#f2f8f5` | ![#0f1c14](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%230f1c14?style=flat-square&color=0f1c14) `#0f1c14` |
| Brand green | ![#3DC97A](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%233DC97A?style=flat-square&color=3DC97A) `#3DC97A` | `#3DC97A` |
| Secondary teal | ![#27C9B8](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%2327C9B8?style=flat-square&color=27C9B8) `#27C9B8` | `#27C9B8` |
| Surface | ![#ffffff](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%23ffffff?style=flat-square&color=ffffff) `#ffffff` | ![#172112](https://img.shields.io/badge/-%23172112?style=flat-square&color=172112) `#172112` |
